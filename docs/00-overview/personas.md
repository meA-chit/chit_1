# Personas

| Persona | Primary goal | Main concerns |
|---|---|---|
| Household Coordinator | Understand upcoming commitments, responsibilities and conflicts without checking multiple services | Completeness, preparation and coordination effort |
| Household Member | See what affects them, what they need to do and when others may be available | Speed, readability and correct interpretation |
| Energy-Aware Homeowner | Relate solar generation, electricity prices and appliance schedules to household plans | Forecast versus actual data and actionable timing |
| Household Administrator | Connect sources, manage deployment, enable modules per member and surface, keep the system reliable | Compatibility, diagnostics, maintainability |
| Privacy-Conscious Member | Control which personal details are visible and who can access them | Shared-screen exposure and sensitive data boundaries |
| Partner with shared finances | Track shared and personal money, split costs fairly, settle up, plan bills and goals (married or unmarried couples, flatmates) | Fairness, privacy between partners, accuracy, no accidental exposure on shared screens |
| Parent / Guardian | Plan school, activities and chores for children; decide what the child's device shows | Child privacy, workload, reliable school information |
| Child | See own school plan, reminders, holidays, sports and family events, and earn stars/allowance for chores | Simplicity, age-appropriate content, motivation |

Personas describe needs and roles, not fixed individuals. A person may take several roles. Partner, Parent and Child personas are new (2026-10-06) and need validation through research.
